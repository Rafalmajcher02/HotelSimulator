- Resolution and Size finalization
	- We shall resize to 1.3 Aspect Ratio (Or simply said for every pixel height, 1.3 pixels wide. If it's 500 high, it'll be 650 wide)
	- Current 1920x1080 UE5 size is 500x650. Remember that larger if this is the resolution you settle on, people with wide and larger monitors will start loosing on details. But if you go too high, the quality might be too big meaning more work and larger file sizes. Personally I think 500x650 is best fit at the moment unless we make a 4K game, but you are free to adjust the resolution as long as the width is exactly x1.30 of height otherwise the logo will look stretched.
