# \# Hotel Simulator

# Prototype / Pre-Alpha

# Windows (PC)

# 

# \## ABOUT THE GAME

# 

# Hotel Simulator is a first-person management simulation game where you build, manage, and expand your own hotel from the ground up. You are responsible for day-to-day operations, hiring and managing staff, and ensuring guests have a comfortable and satisfying stay.

# 

# The game focuses on hands-on management, gradual expansion, and watching your hotel evolve as you make decisions that affect both employees and customers.

# 

# \## GAMEPLAY OVERVIEW

# 

# In Hotel Simulator, you will:

* # Manage and expand your hotel facilities
* # Hire, assign, and oversee NPC employees
* # Handle guest needs and satisfaction
* # Balance efficiency, comfort, and growth
* # Explore and interact with your hotel in first-person

# 

# The core gameplay loop revolves around improving operations, responding to problems as they arise, and reinvesting profits to grow your hotel further.
